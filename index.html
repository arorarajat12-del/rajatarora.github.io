Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does Whimble currently operate?
We currently have personal care attendants across Ottawa and in the west end of the Greater Toronto Area. If you're outside our service area, join our waitlist and we'll notify you when we launch in your city.
How are caregivers vetted?
Every caregiver must submit a vulnerable sector check, provide 2 professional references, hold valid CPR/First Aid certification and professional liability insurance, and pass a live interview reviewing their skills and qualifications.
What happens when I sign up?
After signing up, we schedule a virtual interview to learn about your needs and routines, followed by a home visit to confirm access procedures and equipment. Then you're ready to start requesting care.
Can I choose my caregiver?
Our platform matches you based on your location, needs, and preferences. You can provide feedback after each visit, and we'll prioritize attendants you've had positive experiences with.
What if I need help outside my home?
Whimble supports you wherever life takes you — appointments, errands, events, travel. Our care isn't limited to your home.
